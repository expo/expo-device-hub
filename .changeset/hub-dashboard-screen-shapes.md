Show each device in the screen shape of its model in the Hub dashboard, as on expo.dev: the corners of each iPhone, iPad, Pixel 9, and Pixel 10 model, and the display that an iPhone Duo or a Pixel Pro Fold shows, turned with the device. A device without a known shape shows the whole stream rectangle, with no generic corners. The device frame artwork of the iPhone 17 Pro, the Pixel 9, and the Pixel 10 Pro is removed, and the inspector's "Show device frame" option is now "Show device shape": off, the screen is the whole video rectangle. The option is disabled for a device without a known shape, or whose model the server cannot name, and in the iPhone Duo 3D view, where it does not apply. `InspectorSections` takes `screenShapeAvailable`, and hub-components exports `NO_DEVICE_SHAPE_DESCRIPTION` and `DEVICE_SHAPE_2D_ONLY_DESCRIPTION`. The Hub server sends each device's `model` and no longer sends `deviceFrame`. `Device` and `NewDeviceRequest` take an optional `model`, and `PhoneFrame` and `StreamPanel` take `DeviceShape` from `@expo/hub-client/shapes` as an optional injected prop, so a consumer that does not pass it does not bundle the shapes. `showScreenShape` replaces `showDeviceFrame` and `deviceFrameAssets`, and the `deviceFrame*` exports and `NO_DEVICE_FRAME_DESCRIPTION` are removed.
