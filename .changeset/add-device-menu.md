Adding a device now has two steps. The + button of the simulator or emulator list opens a menu of the recent devices, each with its OS version; a click boots the device. The last row of the menu, "Create new simulator" or "Create new emulator", opens a new modal with the name, the OS version, and the model of the device. A boot that fails opens a dialog with the reason from the host.
