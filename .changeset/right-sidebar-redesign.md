Redesign the right inspector sidebar: bolder collapsible section headers with full-width separators, label/control rows without dividers, select pills for every choice (device settings now use dropdowns instead of segmented controls), compact toggles, and gradient sparkline cards for the Activity and WebRTC charts. The center stream canvas is now gray between white sidebars, separated only by hairline seams. The controls under the device stream are a pill toolbar (Save, Theme, Home, Reload) plus a separate Rotate button with hover tooltips; the Android Back and Recents keys and the shut down / remove actions moved into Device options. Sections animate open and closed with centered titles, and docked sidebars follow the resize handle without easing. The device title and toolbar hug the device frame, and the Current app section uses the same title row and 14 px label/value scale as the other sections. Current app is now collapsible and hosts the activity charts; the inspector order is Current app, Device options, Stream options, Events, Logs, with the first two open by default. Current app lists App ID, Version, and Build number (the minimum OS and PID rows are gone), and Android skips the name and icon line. The first section has no separator above it, and clicking another select while one is open switches menus in a single click. Row labels and select text use a medium (500) weight. The device title pill is larger and semibold and sits farther from the frame, as does the toolbar.
