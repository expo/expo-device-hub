serve-sim now lists paired iPhones and iPads beside the simulators and streams them by hardware UDID, with touch, keyboard, Home, screenshots, and recording. Streaming a device needs Xcode 27 on the Mac and iOS 27 on the device. Features that need a simulator, such as apps, location, and rotation, answer with a reason. When the device disconnects, its session ends.
