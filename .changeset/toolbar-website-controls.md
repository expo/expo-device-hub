The toolbar under the device now has the look of the website's device controls: one rounded bar of round icon buttons, with a dark tooltip above each button that says what it does. An Android device also has Back, Recents, and Dismiss keyboard in the toolbar, next to Home. Take screenshot and the Clipboard menu show a spinner while their action runs. The buttons have new accessible names, such as "Go to home screen", "Take screenshot", "Reload app", and "Rotate device".
