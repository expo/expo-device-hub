The sidebar now has the look of the website's preview settings: sections with a hairline and a title row that takes a fill under the pointer, select boxes of one width, switches that turn blue when on, and pill buttons. The simulator and emulator lists show the platform's mark before each device name, the OS version at the right, and the selected device on a blue surface. The current app is in a card with its icon, name, and app ID.

The inspector no longer adds and removes sections and options while a device connects or reconnects. A selected device always has the sections and options of its platform. An option that cannot change now keeps its last value and is locked, and a tooltip gives the reason. An option whose value is not known shows a dash.
