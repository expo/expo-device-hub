The device screen loads like the screen on the Expo website. A selected device shows its shape at once, in its real proportions. Until the stream connects, the black screen shows a glow that moves around its edge, and the first frame fades in above it. A session that fails before its first frame says why on the screen. The dot of the status pill pulses while the status is Loading, Reconnecting, or Live.
